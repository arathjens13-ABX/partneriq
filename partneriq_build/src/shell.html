File naming conventions Offline build: no internet/CDN/font calls. CSV upload and preloaded sharing work fully locally. TV Signage: any filename containing "TV", "signage", or "visible" (one file, all brands in Brand column)
Organic Social: filenames starting with BrandedPartnerPerformance, BrandedContentSeries, or BrandOnAsset — add a ReportDate column (YYYY-MM-DD)
Survey (brand recall): filename starting with Survey_ (one file, all brands + all waves stacked)
Fan Insights (general survey questions): filename starting with GeneralSurvey_ — columns: Question, Answer Count, Frequency, Percentage, Rank, Survey, Season, Sheet, Date, Responses
Partner survey questions (Moda Health / Delta Dental): filename starting with ModaDeltaDental_ — columns: Question, Q_, Response, Frequency, Percentage, Survey, Date, Responses
Programs & community: filename starting with Program_ — columns: Brand (program name), Frequency, Q, Question, Survey, Date, Responses, Answer Percentage
Partner roster: filename starting with Partners_ — columns: Account, Category
Paid Social: any filename containing "paid", "meta", "facebook", or "admanager". Campaign names are parsed locally; unclear partner assignments can be reviewed manually.
Brand aliases: use Manage brand aliases to merge naming variants like Daimler Trucks North America into one canonical partner.
Organic Social: BrandName_DateRange.csv — one file per brand
Drop multiple CSV files at once. All processing happens locally in your browser — offline mode makes no external calls.

Review paid campaign assignments

Campaigns with medium or low confidence can be assigned to an existing partner or a new partner. Assignments are saved as reusable rules and included when you export a preloaded dashboard.

Review General Survey assignments

Each answer option from GeneralSurvey_ files is shown with its auto-detected partner link. Override any assignment or explicitly mark options as "not a partner mention." Rules are saved and included when you export a preloaded dashboard.

Manage brand aliases

Merge source names that should roll up into one partner across TV, paid, organic, and future channels. Example: Daimler Trucks North America → Daimler. Alias rules are included when you export a preloaded dashboard.
